Controls & shortcuts
Space play/pause · ← → seek 1/16 (Shift = measure) · M mute · drag the ruler or waveform to scrub · L loop · 1 beams 2 bloom 3 shadows 4 strobes · C camera · O orbit · [ ] fixture count · S stats · ` controls panel · H haze
Ctrl/⌘ + wheel zoom at pointer · Ctrl/⌘ +/- zoom · Ctrl/⌘ 0 fit · Middle-drag the timeline or waveform to pan · D drop mode · V select mode · Backspace deletes the selection
Drop mode: drag a lane to draw a clip, drag a clip to move, drag its edge to resize · Select mode: click or marquee cards, drag a selected card to move the selection, drag a card edge to resize it, Shift/Ctrl + click adds, Ctrl/⌘ A selects all, Ctrl/⌘ C copies, Ctrl/⌘ V pastes at the playhead, Ctrl/⌘ Shift V pastes at the pointer and hovered track; right-click a lane and choose Paste here for the same spot; right-click a clip for Copy/Delete. A dashed line and row tint show where here is whenever the clipboard has clips
Shift + drag anywhere in the timeline to draw a loop · drag the cyan loop edges to adjust it · L or the Loop button toggles looping · Fit loop zooms to it
Click a fixture name to open its side panel (click again to close) · Playback loops measures 41–56 (1 measure = 1 bar of 4 beats); scrubbing before the loop plays the intro through to the loop end.
